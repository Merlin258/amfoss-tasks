## Something you've learned outside a classroom or formal course. How did you learn it?
One of the most important skills i have learned outside of the classroom is the art of faking it till making it. This skill is the salt to our world; by itself it's a bit sketchy, but pair it with knowledge and suddenly you are the most well versed man about a topic in the room. I have seen a lot of people in the industry claiming that no one knows fully about everything. But society does not perceive honesty as power; it perceives knowledge/concreteness as the absolute safety. Between two people with similar knowledge, a person with more confidence/"the fake it till you make it" factor is almost certainly bound to climb the corporate ladder even if their knowledge base is slightly smaller. Even if you follow a hard core programming-over-corporate, while working with teams, people are bound to trust the opinion of the confident guy. Faking to make it is not simply just a cunning tactic, it is one of the most effective ways to skill up while minimizing one's imposter syndrome. Sure you do fake it. but at the end you also make it; and that's the most important part.
